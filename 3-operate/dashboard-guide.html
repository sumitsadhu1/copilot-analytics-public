1. Copilot Dashboard Pages

The Copilot Dashboard is accessible at insights.cloud.microsoft and is divided into three main pages: Readiness, Adoption, and Impact, each with a Sentiment section; a Learning tab carries Microsoft research. Each page answers a different question about your organisation’s Copilot deployment.

1.1 Readiness Page

The Readiness page answers: “How many Copilot licences have we assigned, how many users are active, and what should we enable next?”

  • “Copilot licenses assigned” and “Active Copilot users” — the metrics in the “Activate Copilot for your organization” section summarise progress towards activating Copilot. Tenant-level counts include disabled mailboxes; group-level counts do not.
  • Enablement recommendations — “Steps to enable Copilot and accelerate your AI transformation” cards for Copilot Chat, Microsoft Copilot and agents. Managers do not have access to the Readiness page, and delegates do not see this section. Microsoft’s Supported environments matrix lists the Readiness page as not available in GCC Moderate or GCC High.
  • Not a full readiness check — Microsoft states that the tab does not provide a comprehensive summary of all readiness and eligibility requirements.
Feature Availability by Licence Count

Tenants with 1+ assigned Copilot licences get core Readiness, Adoption, and Impact capabilities. Microsoft lists agent-related insights, benchmarks, intelligent summaries, Pulse and Glint survey-based sentiment, week- and month-level trendlines, the group-level view for managers and delegation, and the “All” licence-type view as requiring at least 50 Copilot or 50 Viva Insights licences. For capabilities using the 50-plan threshold, Microsoft’s Environment requirements for Viva Insights (Last updated 12 May 2026) explicitly counts the Viva Insights service plan included with Copilot and gives 30 Copilot + 20 Viva Insights = 50 qualifying service plans. The Dashboard-specific Connect to the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard for Microsoft 365 customers (Last updated 29 September 2026) instead says 50 Copilot or 50 Viva Insights licences. Do not assume a mixed deployment fails: verify assigned service plans, feature presence, and processing in the tenant; escalate to Microsoft Support if the documented qualifying mix does not unlock the capability.

For Agent Dashboard (preview), conservatively require 50 assigned Microsoft 365 Copilot licences plus qualifying agent activity. Microsoft’s feature-specific Agent article conflicts with the broader Dashboard article on this prerequisite.

1.2 Adoption Page

The Adoption page answers: “Who is using Copilot, how often, and in which apps?”

Key Metrics

MetricDefinition
Active Copilot usersNumber of licensed users who had at least one Copilot interaction in the 28-day measurement window.
Copilot Actions TakenTotal count of Copilot actions across supported Microsoft 365 apps and Copilot Chat. Agent adoption is reported separately in the Agent Dashboard, but Microsoft states that Teams Facilitator and Interpreter agent usage is included in overall usage metrics.
Usage IntensityAverage number of actions per active user (Actions Taken ÷ Active Users). Measures depth of engagement beyond simple headcount.
Returning usersUsers who took at least one Copilot action in both the current and the preceding period (monthly or weekly toggle). Shows sustained adoption rather than one-time trial.
Usage levelsPower, Habitual, Novice and Other users, classified over a rolling 12-week window — see Usage Levels (Power Users) below.
Internal BenchmarkAn additional adoption trendline for “a group of similar employees within your company”. Select Show benchmark to display it.
External BenchmarkCompares your adoption trends with “Copilot usage at other companies similar to yours”. Helps answer “Are we ahead or behind?”

Usage Levels (Power Users)

The Adoption page classifies licensed users over a rolling 12-week window. In the section that analyses the drivers of power usage, the gear icon at the top right of the Frequent usage card lets you customise the number of actions used to group users.

LevelDefinition (Microsoft)
Power usersAverage 15 or more Copilot actions per week and used Copilot in at least 9 of the past 12 weeks
Habitual usersAverage between 1 and 14 Copilot actions per week and used Copilot in at least 9 of the past 12 weeks
Novice usersAverage at least 1 Copilot action in the past 12 weeks and did not use Copilot in at least 9 of the past 12 weeks
Other usersLicensed users who meet none of the criteria above, including users who have not yet used Copilot in the measurement window

Microsoft’s Supported environments matrix lists power users metrics as available in Worldwide, not available in GCC Moderate and partial in GCC High.

Use Copilot Chat as the current reporting metric. Do not interpret a decline in the Copilot Chat (legacy web) series as declining adoption by itself; Microsoft is merging current activity into Copilot Chat, so the legacy series is expected to approach zero.

“View by: Group” Shows Only One Person?

If a leader selects “View by: Group” and sees only themselves (or far fewer people than expected), the most common cause is missing intermediate managers in the org data. The manager hierarchy chain must include a row for every manager between the leader and their reports. Verify that your CSV (or Entra Manager field) has rows for every intermediate manager. Gaps break the chain and orphan the downstream employees from the leader’s view.

Suppressed Values (“--”)

When you see “--” instead of a number, it means the group size is below the minimum group size threshold (default: 10). This is a privacy protection, not an error. Data is present but suppressed to prevent identification of individuals. If you need to see data for smaller teams, an Insights Admin can adjust the minimum group size in privacy settings — but this should be done carefully with HR and legal approval.

Agent-Related Insight Cards on the Adoption Page (Preview)

In addition to the core Adoption insights (overview, usage intensity, returning users, per-app breakdown), the Adoption page now surfaces two agent-focused insight cards when your tenant has agent activity. These are independent of the Agent Dashboard itself and appear on the Adoption page for tenants with at least 50 Copilot or 50 Viva Insights licences; agent-related insights are not included with 1–49 Copilot licences.

CardWhat It ShowsRequires
Agent adoption by Copilot users (preview) The number of Copilot users who have used at least one agent, the number of agents with at least one active Copilot user, and the top three agents sorted by active users (with change-over-time and agent-response counts). Lets you jump directly into the agent deep-dive page from the Adoption page. At least one active agent user in the tenant. No PAYG required.
Credit usage overview Copilot Credits consumed by Copilot Chat users, the number of agents those credits were used across, and the number of Copilot Chat users driving credit consumption. Also breaks out the top groups driving credit usage (by group / organisation / job function) with 28-day change. Your tenant must have Pay-As-You-Go enabled for Copilot Studio / Copilot Chat. Without PAYG the card does not appear.
Why these cards matter even before you open the Agent Dashboard

Both cards live on the regular Adoption page, so leaders who never opened the Agent Dashboard can still see whether agents are being used and whether they cost money. The Agent adoption by Copilot users card is the easiest way for a CIO to answer “are people actually trying our agents yet?”. The Credit usage overview card is the first place to spot a runaway PAYG bill before it shows up in the billing report. Click into either card to jump to the corresponding deep-dive view in the Agent Dashboard. The Agent adoption card is tagged as a public-preview feature — UI may shift before GA. Microsoft’s article does not label the Credit usage overview card as preview.

1.3 Impact Page

The Impact page answers: “What value is Copilot delivering?”

Check diagnostic coverage before interpreting impact

Optional diagnostic data affects Copilot assisted hours, emails sent using Copilot, Teams meetings summarized or recapped, meeting hours summarized or recapped, and Teams chats summarized. Required diagnostic data still provides a subset, so the metrics may populate while remaining materially undercounted. Record the tenant’s optional diagnostic-data policy and keep it stable across comparison periods.

Source: Connect to the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard for Microsoft 365 customers (Last updated 29 September 2026), Impact section.

Top-Level Metrics

MetricDefinitionHow It’s Calculated
Copilot Actions TakenTotal Copilot interactions in the periodDirect count of all Copilot actions (same as Adoption page)
Copilot Assisted HoursEstimated hours saved by CopilotMeeting summaries + search/summaries valued at 6 min each + content creation valued at 6 min each
Copilot Assisted ValueDollar value of time savedAssisted Hours × hourly rate (default $72/hr). Hourly rate is session-only — changes are not saved between visits.
Satisfaction RateUser satisfaction with Copilot responsesThumbs up ÷ (thumbs up + thumbs down) reactions, 28-day rolling window. No survey required — based on in-app feedback. Shown only with at least 30 responses from at least five users.
Estimate, not causal proof

Assisted Hours and Assisted Value are modeled estimates using Microsoft’s assumptions and the hourly rate entered for the current session. They are not realized cash savings and do not establish that Copilot caused an outcome. For decision-grade analysis, document the assumptions, use a stable baseline and diagnostic-data policy, compare suitable cohorts or periods, and pair usage measures with independently governed business outcome data.

Current documented metric issue and incident validation

The current controlling Dashboard article documents the email issue below. A previously documented meeting issue is not present in that article as of 2 October 2026, so do not carry that earlier qualifier into a current report without a dated source.

Metric CategoryAffected PeriodDetails
Email (Outlook Copilot)June 1, 2025 – February 18, 2026Generate email draft and Summarise email thread active users and actions taken, plus Copilot assisted hours and Copilot assisted value, underreported. Fix applied from February 18, 2026; historical data restoration pending. All-up user counts for Microsoft Copilot and Copilot in Outlook are not affected. Advanced reporting Power BI reports and custom queries are affected too.

Do not automatically attribute a dip to this issue. Microsoft’s metric reference also notes that some feature-level and app-level counts for Word, Excel, Outlook and PowerPoint might decline from late April or early May 2026 while conversational prompts are separated from edit-mode prompts, and that total and app-level counts continue to capture edit-mode prompts. Read both statements before attributing a dip. Check Microsoft 365 admin center Service Health for current incidents and Message Center for rollout or metric-change notices, then compare the incident dates, cloud, apps, and affected metrics with the observed pattern.

Export Copilot Metrics Safely (Preview)

The Copilot metrics export is a preview feature with a fixed, non-customizable schema. It exports user-level metric rows (anonymised by default), not the aggregated chart currently visible on screen.

Prerequisites

  • The tenant must meet the documented 50-licence threshold. Because Microsoft pages conflict on mixed service-plan counting, verify assigned service plans and export availability in the tenant.
  • The exporter must have global / Your company access. A manager or “Your group” delegate cannot export.
  • The Copilot Metrics Export feature must be enabled for the user, and the capability must be available in the tenant’s cloud. It is not available in GCC Moderate or GCC High according to Microsoft’s Supported environments matrix.

How to Export

  1. Open the Copilot Dashboard in the Viva Insights web app.
  2. Select the Copilot metrics Export control.
  3. Choose Week for weekly rows covering the past six months or Day for daily rows covering the past 28 days.
  4. Download the CSV and record the export date, granularity, source article date, and tenant/cloud used.

What’s in the Export

PropertyCorrect interpretation
Grain and periodOne user-level record per time interval (anonymised by default): up to six months for Week, or 28 days for Day.
IdentityDe-identified by default: personal identifiers are replaced with anonymised IDs, so the export is user-level data, not aggregate-only data. When identifiable export is enabled for a user, their export includes identifiable fields. Microsoft lists Identification for the Copilot Dashboard data export (private preview); access is governed by a dedicated Viva feature access management (VFAM) policy, which only a Microsoft 365 Global Administrator or an AI Administrator can create.
CustomizationThe schema and metric selection are not customizable, and Dashboard filters/groupings do not redefine the export.
MetricsThe documented export metrics are provided in the fixed schema. Copilot Assisted Hours is not included. Day-level exports also omit the meeting-recap and meeting-summary metrics. Zero values can appear because the export covers users with and without a Copilot licence; use Total Copilot enabled days to identify enabled users.

Privacy Handling

  • Treat the CSV as sensitive analytics data even though direct identities are removed by default (an export with identifiable export enabled contains personal data); repeated user-level rows and joins with organizational data can increase re-identification risk.
  • Store it in an access-controlled location, apply retention and sensitivity requirements, restrict downstream joins, and document every recipient and purpose.
  • Do not present row-level data or attempt re-identification. Aggregate under approved minimum-group and privacy rules before wider distribution.
  • Validate row counts, dates, granularity, metric definitions, missing values, and diagnostic-data coverage before publishing trends.
Export vs Advanced Analytics

The preview export provides a fixed user-level dataset (anonymised by default). Use Advanced Reporting when an Insights Analyst needs governed metric selection, partition-scoped queries, or custom segmentation. Neither route removes the need for privacy review.

Source: Export Copilot metrics from the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard (Last updated 15 July 2026).

2. How to Delegate Access

Dashboard access can be shared with others through delegation. This is how leaders give their managers or analysts visibility into Copilot usage for their scope.

Three Methods to Add Delegates

MethodSteps
Method 1: SettingsViva Insights web app → Settings (left navigation) → Delegation → Add delegates → enter name(s) → Confirm
Method 2: ShareCopilot Dashboard or Agent Dashboard → Share (top right) → Add delegates → enter name(s) → Confirm
Method 3: Reports pageReports page → Add delegates → enter name(s) → Confirm

A senior leader who has company-level access and is a manager chooses each delegate’s scope: Your group, Your company or Combined access. Combined access includes the Consumption Dashboard, and a delegate with it sees exactly what the delegator sees. A delegate can’t delegate further. The maximum is 75 delegates. Delegates must also be permitted to use the dashboard. If the delegator has both company-level and group access, existing “Your company” delegates automatically become Combined access and might see the Consumption Dashboard. Review delegate scopes after this change. See Microsoft’s Delegate access article.

Delegation Rules

RuleDetail
Maximum delegates75 per delegator
No cascadingDelegation does not flow downward. If Alice delegates to Bob, Bob cannot re-delegate to Carol.
“Your group” scopeDelegate sees the delegator’s reporting chain only
“Your company” scopeDelegate sees the entire tenant — use sparingly. If the delegator also has group (manager) access, existing “Your company” delegates automatically become Combined access and might see the Consumption Dashboard.
“Combined access” scopeAvailable only if the delegator has both company-level and group manager access. Includes the Consumption Dashboard. The delegate sees exactly what the delegator sees.
Agent Dashboard accessRequires “Your company” or “Combined access” scope. “Your group” delegates cannot see the Agent Dashboard.
Delegator leaves orgAll delegates immediately lose access. Recovery: another leader must re-delegate to those users.
Admin controlAdmins can disable delegation for the Copilot Dashboard or Agent Dashboard for the tenant, a group or a user with a Viva feature access policy; changes can take up to 24 hours to apply.
AuditDelegate assignments can be audited in the Purview audit log (AddDelegates and RemoveDelegates operations).
Plan for Delegator Departures

If a key delegator (e.g., a VP or CIO) leaves the organisation, all 75 delegates could lose dashboard access simultaneously. Document who has delegated to whom and ensure at least two senior leaders have overlapping delegation lists for continuity.

5. Agent Dashboard

The Agent Dashboard provides visibility into supported agent activity (agents built in Copilot Studio, SharePoint or the Microsoft 365 Agents Toolkit, by end users, or by Microsoft and third parties) for eligible users with “Your company” scope. Microsoft applies a public-preview label to this surface — see the preview-scope callout below, where the two governing articles disagree on how far that label reaches. “Your group” delegates do not receive Agent Dashboard access. Microsoft’s articles are inconsistent on managers: the Agent Dashboard and Delegate access articles say enabled managers have manager-level access scoped to their teams, while Roles in Viva Insights and the Copilot Analytics introduction list Agent Dashboard access for analysts with global partition access and senior leaders only. Validate manager access in the tenant before promising or withholding it.

Two dashboard views — confirm which one a figure came from

The Agent Dashboard now contains two distinct reporting views, switched at the top right: select All agents for the Agent 365 Dashboard; clear All agents for the Copilot Agent Dashboard. Metrics and reporting history differ between them. Record which view was selected alongside any figure you export, and do not compare figures across views or blend them into one report without labelling the view.

Three Practical Scenarios

Scenario 1: Post-Launch Adoption Review

Your organisation deployed three custom agents last month. Use the Agent Dashboard to answer:

  • How many users actually tried each agent? (Active users per agent)
  • Are people coming back? (Returning users)
  • Which agent is getting the most traction? (Most popular agent)

Action: Double down on training for the popular agent; investigate why low-adoption agents are being ignored (discoverability? relevance?).

Scenario 2: Cost Governance for Pay-As-You-Go

Your tenant uses PAYG billing for agent interactions. Use the Agent Dashboard to answer:

  • How many Copilot Credits are being consumed? (Credits metric, License filter set to Copilot Chat)
  • Which agents are the biggest consumers? (Sort by responses)
  • Are third-party agents driving unexpected costs? (Filter by creator type: Third party)

Action: Set credit alerts, review high-consumption agents with their owners, consider blocking non-essential agents.

Scenario 3: Shadow AI Detection

Users are creating their own agents without IT approval. Use the Agent Dashboard to answer:

  • How many user-created agents exist? (Filter by creator type: User)
  • Are any user-created agents being widely shared? (Most shared agent)
  • Do any of these agents have external data connections that violate policy?

Action: Follow the Detect → Act workflow below.

Check first: a Viva feature access control can exclude non-public agents (creator type User or Your org) from the Agent 365 Dashboard view, which would hide the agents this scenario looks for. All agents are included by default.
